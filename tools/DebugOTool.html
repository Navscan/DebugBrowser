🐞 DebugOTool v2.0

Inject the debug bar + panel into any staging page.

📌 Step 1: Host the script

Place DebugOTool.js and DebugOTool.css in your staging folder.
Example: /var/www/staging.treasuretrail/tools/

📋 Step 2: Create the Bookmarklet

Copy the code below, create a new bookmark in your browser, and paste it into the URL field.

javascript:(function(){var s=document.createElement('script');s.src='https://staging.treasuretrail.africa/tools/DebugOTool.js';document.head.appendChild(s);})();

📱 Step 3: Download DebugBrowser APK

Get the native Android app with built‑in debugging:

⬇️ Download APK

🚀 Step 4: How to use it

  1. Open your staging Onboarding page (https://staging.treasuretrail.africa/onboarding).
  2. Tap your browser bookmarks and select "DebugOTool".
  3. The gold debug bar and 🐞 button will instantly appear on the page!
